Privacy Policy

Deal Tracker · Last updated September 7, 2026

Deal Tracker (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is an independent iOS app for timeshare sales representatives to scan, log, and track their own deals and commissions. This policy explains what the app stores, who processes it, and how you can control it.

Deal Tracker is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any resort or timeshare company. Reps use it for their own personal records.

1. Who uses this app

Deal Tracker is used by sales representatives (“you”). When you photograph or enter a sales form, you are entering information about your customers. We store that information on your behalf. You are responsible for handling your customers’ information in line with your own legal and employer obligations.

2. Information we store

  • Account information: the email address you use to sign in.
  • Deal records: the details you scan or enter for each deal. These include your customers’ personal information, such as names, phone numbers, account numbers, and purchase, financing, and commission figures.
  • Scanned form images: the photos you take of sales forms. They are kept in private storage and can only be opened through short-lived signed links from your signed-in account.
  • Usage telemetry: basic events such as when a scan is started or finished, used to keep the app working and to understand how it is used. Telemetry never includes form contents or customer details.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location data, or your device contacts.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide the app: reading deal data from your photos and showing you your deals, commissions, and related totals. Each account can access only its own data.

We never sell your data or your customers’ data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models.

4. Where data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase on servers located in the United States. If you use the app from outside the United States, your data is transferred to and stored there.

5. Service providers

The app relies on these providers to work. Each receives only what it needs for its role.

  • Supabase — hosts the database, sign-in, and private image storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — when you scan a form, the image is sent to Anthropic’s API to read the printed fields and turn them into a deal record. Anthropic processes the image to provide this service to us.
  • Apple — handles all payments and subscriptions through the App Store. Your card details stay with Apple and never reach us.
  • RevenueCat — processes your subscription status so the app knows whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat does not receive card numbers either.

6. Your customers’ data

The customer information in your deal records belongs to your customers and is entered by you. We store it on your behalf so you can keep your own records. You are responsible for making sure you may record it and for handling it consistently with your legal and employer obligations. We never sell it, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete individual deals in the app, which removes the record and its image.

To delete your account and everything in it, open Settings → Delete account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, deal records, and scanned images are removed.

To request an export of your data, or for any other data question, email help@repdealtracker.app.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Records are protected by access controls so each account can only reach its own data, and form images live in private storage that is only reachable through signed links. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children

The app is for professional use by adults and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the most recent revision.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email help@repdealtracker.app.
