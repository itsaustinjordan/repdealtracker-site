Support

Deal Tracker · Last updated September 7, 2026

Need help with Deal Tracker, or have a question? Email help@repdealtracker.app. We usually reply within a few business days.

To help us sort things out quickly, include the app version (shown in Settings), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please do not include customer details in your email.

Subscriptions and billing

Deal Tracker includes 5 free scans. After that, scanning needs a subscription of $5.99 per month or $59.99 per year, billed through your Apple ID.

  • Cancel: open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Deal Tracker. Your access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Refunds: Apple handles all refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restore a purchase: if you reinstalled the app or moved to a new phone, sign in with the same Apple ID and use the restore option on the subscription screen.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, deal records, and scanned images are removed. You do not need to email us.

Exporting your data

Email help@repdealtracker.app from the address on your account and we will send you an export of your deals.

Scans that read wrong

Deal details are read from your photos automatically, and faint print, glare, or handwriting can cause mistakes. Always check the figures against the original form. If scans keep failing or misreading, email us with the app version and a description of what went wrong.
